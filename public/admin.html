Fishers

Registered fishers sign in to the app with their mobile number and a 4-digit PIN, and can record a landing by calling the Fish Passport number from that mobile. Harbour and boat are filled in automatically for phone landings. Type a new PIN and press Save to reset one.

Transporters

Transporters sign in with their mobile number and PIN, then pick up and deliver batches. Each pickup and delivery is confirmed by the other side. Type a new PIN and press Save to reset one.
